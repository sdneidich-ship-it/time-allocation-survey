The purpose of this Time Allocation Survey is to understand how you used your time in the past, how you use it today, and how you would ideally use it in the future.

For each view, think of a typical two-week period. The target is 80 working hours, but enter more if that is what a typical two weeks really looks like. Split your hours across the activities below. It takes about 10 minutes.

Historical ideal

A time when you feel your time was well allocated and it led to success.

Current normal

A typical, or median, two weeks.

Future ideal

If you had a magic wand and could reasonably allocate your time the way you feel would be most effective, what would it look like?
